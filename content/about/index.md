I’m an applied AI engineer at OpenAI. I work with large enterprises and digital-native companies, drawing on the full range of OpenAI products. Two questions interest me in particular. How could a Company OS help teams share what they know and coordinate their day-to-day work? And how could a software factory use AI to take a change from issue to release, with engineers deciding what ships?

I joined in July 2026 after nearly four years at GitLab. I started there in customer success and left as a Staff AI Solutions Architect, the specialist for the Duo Agent Platform in the sales organisation.

In 1988 my father handed down his Commodore 64. Four friends and I spent hours with our noses inches from the buzzing TV, taking turns at [Mafia](https://www.c64-wiki.com/wiki/Mafia). Somewhere between loading games from cassette and looking after the computers at a mechanical engineering institute in Aachen, people started paying me to do this. For a while I was also flipping burgers on the night shift. Later, running support teams in Athens gave me a lasting interest in what happens when a job fails at two in the morning.

That interest turned into a [pipeline triage demo](https://github.com/universalamateur/Codex-Pipeline-Triage). Give it a failed GitLab pipeline and it brings an explanation back to the merge request. Opening a fix is a button I left for the human. I’ve also built two MCP servers for my own use. One lets me manage my calendar in [Reclaim](https://gitlab.com/universalamateur1/reclaim-mcp-server). The other helps me find things I know I wrote down somewhere in [my Markdown notes](https://gitlab.com/universalamateur1/zettelkasten-mcp).

On my Mac I’m starting a smaller experiment. I wrote two sentences about a fictional web standard, then three claims about it. One claim says two browsers shipped it. Can a small model spot that neither sentence mentions a browser? I have the test cases. I haven’t run the models yet.

I keep the [build notes on my blog](https://universalamateur.gitlab.io/post/). A good place to start is [why I built a pricing calculator that works offline](https://universalamateur.gitlab.io/post/building-an-air-gapped-pricing-calculator-with-zero-backend/).
